This position paper argues that self-evolving LLM systems fail when the synthetic data they generate carries no learnable information gain across iterations. We propose three design principles for sustained self-improvement beyond the initial self-play plateau: asymmetric co-evolution across Proposer, Solver, and Verifier roles; capacity growth; and proactive information seeking.
