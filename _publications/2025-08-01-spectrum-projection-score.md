This paper introduces Spectrum Projection Score (SPS), a metric that measures how well retrieved information aligns with a reader language model's internal representations without requiring labelled data. Building on SPS, we propose xCompress, a framework that selectively samples and ranks retrieval summaries to improve retrieval-augmented generation across multiple question-answering benchmarks.
